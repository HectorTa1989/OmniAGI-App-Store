Privacy Policy

OmniAGI is designed to keep local work local unless you choose a provider or cloud feature that needs to receive it.

Who operates OmniAGI

OmniAGI is operated by Hector Ta. Privacy questions can be sent to hectorta1989@outlook.com.

Data stored on your device

The app stores conversations, settings, provider profiles, generated-image history, and imported content on your device. Provider API keys and connected-device credentials are stored using iOS Keychain. Local data leaves the device only when you use a feature that requires a provider, choose cloud Sync, share or export content, or use an operating system service such as dictation.

Data processed by your chosen AI providers

When you send a prompt, attachment, image request, or live voice session, the content needed for that request is sent directly to the provider endpoint you configured. That provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy. OmniAGI does not control a provider's retention or model-training practices. Review them before adding a key.

Optional OmniAGI account and Sync

If you connect an OmniAGI account, we process account identifiers such as email address, display name, and internal user ID. If you enable Sync, we store the conversations and attachments you choose to synchronize, revision metadata, device access-key hashes, and account subscription status so Sync and recovery can work. Cloud content is not end-to-end encrypted.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. OmniAGI receives transaction identifiers, product identifiers, subscription state, purchase and expiration dates, revocation state, and environment information needed to confirm Pro access and prevent a purchase from being linked to multiple accounts. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Microphone, speech, photos, and files

Microphone access is used only after you start dictation or a voice call. Device dictation may be processed by Apple according to your device settings. Live-call audio is sent to the provider you configured. Photo and file access is used only for items you select or explicitly save.

Diagnostics and security

Our hosting and security infrastructure may process standard technical data such as IP address, request time, route, device or browser metadata, and error details to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. Do not include secrets in support messages.

How data is used

We use data to provide requested app features, authenticate accounts and devices, synchronize content, validate subscriptions, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, maintain security, and comply with law. We do not sell personal data or use app content for third-party advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it or remove the app, subject to device backups. Synced content remains while your account needs it for the service or until deletion, subject to limited security, backup, dispute, and legal retention. You can delete local conversations in the app and contact us to request account or cloud-data deletion.

Children

OmniAGI is not directed to children under 13. Provider services may impose higher minimum ages. A parent or guardian should review the selected provider's terms before allowing a minor to use the app.

International processing and changes

Service providers may process data in countries other than your own, with protections required by applicable law. We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
